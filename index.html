Fixation: privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Fixation is a free game about bacteria. This page says what it does with your information: in short, nothing leaves your device.

What the game collects

Nothing. Fixation has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network connections of its own. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information, from adults or from children.

What stays on your device

The game saves your current run, your progress (species and difficulty levels opened) and your settings (sound, music, speed) in its own storage on your device, or in your browser's storage when you play on the web. This never leaves your device, and uninstalling the game or clearing your browser data deletes it.

Tips

Tips are optional and unlock nothing. On Android they are bought through Google Play, and on the web through GitHub Sponsors. Those services handle the payment under their own privacy policies; the game itself never sees your payment details. On Android, Google Play tells the game only whether a tip went through, so it can say thank you.

Where you download it

The store you get Fixation from (Google Play or itch.io) may collect information under its own privacy policy, for example download counts.

Children

Fixation is suitable for children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact
